# Yiwen Wang

I am a PhD candidate in Media and Communications at the University of Sydney. My research examines platform governance, cultural production, and the formalisation of grey-zone cultural practices on Chinese short-video platforms.

My doctoral project focuses on film commentary short videos on Douyin. It explores how platforms mediate relationships between creators, copyright holders, cultural industries, and state regulation.

My broader research interests include platform studies, digital cultural labour, screen culture, short-video industries, short-drama platforms, and media governance in China.

## Research Interests

- Film commentary short videos
- Digital cultural labour

- Chinese digital media and cultural regulation

## Current Research

### Platformising the Grey Zone

My doctoral research investigates how film commentary short videos on Douyin have moved from informal and legally ambiguous cultural practices toward platform-mediated forms of formalisation and industrialisation.

The project examines how creators, platforms, copyright holders, and state regulators negotiate the boundaries of legitimacy, creativity, and value in China’s platformised cultural industries.

### Informal Labour and Platform Governance

I am also working on a related project on live-stream clipping workers on Douyin. This project examines how informal cultural labour is absorbed into platform infrastructures while remaining institutionally unrecognised and precarious.
